Design principles

The project is still early, but its central constraints are already clear.

Explicit authority​

Identity, actor, customer context, capability, approval, and execution admission are separate facts. Convenience does not collapse them into ambient permission.

Evidence before claims​

Status language follows executable evidence. A design, development contract, working skeleton, and stable production release are different things and are labeled differently.

Refuse rather than weaken​

If a governed path requires confinement, an attested adapter, or a specific capability grant, the system refuses when that property is absent. It does not silently fall back to a weaker path.

Immutable inputs, reversible change​

Runs bind immutable revisions and authority snapshots. Improvements enter as candidates. Promotions and rollbacks are explicit decisions with an attributable basis.

Domain ownership​

The component with the deepest semantic knowledge owns the rule. Provider semantics belong in Connectors, execution mechanics in Substrate, lifecycle ordering in Agent, and customer policy in products.

Deployment without semantic drift​

Personal, organizational, and hosted postures may differ in topology and operation, but they should not invent different domain rules or quietly remove safety properties.

Bounded everything​

Buffers, payloads, processes, retries, queues, event retention, cleanup, and cancellation all need explicit limits. “Eventually” is not an operational bound.
