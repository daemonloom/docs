Failure and recovery

Failure handling is part of governance. A system that carefully authorizes the first attempt but retries through an unbounded fallback has not preserved the boundary.

Keep failure typed​

Failure classExampleSafe direction
Authoritymissing or expired grantrefuse and request new authority
Decisiondenied or expired approvalstop or create a fresh proposal
Capabilityrequired browser or confinement unavailablerefuse; do not weaken placement
Dependencyprovider or service unavailableretry only under operation policy
Lifecycleinvalid transition or stale tool resultreject the transition
Uncertain effectresponse lost after a non-idempotent writereconcile before repeating
Resourcebudget, time, storage, or process bound reachedstop within the declared terminal path

Typed outcomes let products explain what happened and choose a recovery without inventing authority.

Retry depends on contract facts​

Idempotent operations can often be retried under a bounded policy. Conditional operations require the named condition. Non-idempotent operations need reconciliation or an operation-specific strategy when the first effect is uncertain.

Backoff, attempt count, deadline, and budget are finite. A retry does not extend an expired approval or resurrect a revoked grant.

Cancellation is an ordered outcome​

Cancellation requests propagate through the run lifecycle and any owned execution boundary. A caller should distinguish “cancellation requested,” “work stopped,” and “terminal cancellation observed.” External effects already accepted by a provider may not be reversible.

Recovery preserves the audit trail​

Recovery creates new attributable work: a reconciliation read, compensating operation, replacement run, or rollback decision. It does not edit history until the earlier attempt appears successful.

Refuse rather than bypass​

Missing confinement does not become host execution. Missing Connector authority does not become a raw vendor token. A control-plane outage does not become cached approval unless a bounded offline policy explicitly describes that mode.
