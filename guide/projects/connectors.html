Connectors

Status: pre-v1 foundation phase; the catalog family builds, the deployable platform remains design work.

Connectors is the unified integration boundary for agent automation: a text-declared connector catalog compiled into canonical artifacts, plus a planned service that owns identity binding, connections, credentials, grants, invocation, and event delivery.

Why it exists​

An agent should not receive a bag of credentials and reconstruct provider behavior on its own. Connectors turns vendor APIs and event sources into declared capabilities, and binds every use to a narrow, reviewable grant.

Deployment posture​

The design supports personal, organization, and SaaS postures with the same feature semantics. Topology changes; the core capability and authority model does not.
